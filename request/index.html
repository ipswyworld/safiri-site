WhatsApp is usually the quickest way to reach you once you are travelling.

Free, and it books nothing. This opens your email app with the request written out.

What happens next

  1. You tell us the trip

    Where, when, how many of you, and the kind of help you want.

  2. We reply within 24 hours

    With a guide whose licence we have checked: their name, what they charge, and what the price leaves out, such as park entry fees. If nobody we have checked is free on your dates, we tell you that instead.

  3. You agree it with the guide

    You confirm the plan with them and pay them directly. During the pilot Safiri takes nothing from you or from the guide.

Park and reserve fees are paid at the gate. See current entry fees.
