Two apps

Booking a trip and working a trip are different jobs, so they are different apps. Neither one signs the other's people in.

For travellers

Safiri

Find somewhere to go, see what a guide charges and what it excludes, book, and follow each other on the day so you actually find one another.

  • Places with real costs, park fees separated out
  • Guides whose licences have been checked
  • Live location only while your trip is running
  • Pay your guide directly, with the full price shown first

Android, in testing. iOS to follow.

For guides and drivers

Safiri Partner

Your licences, your calendar, what you charge, and what you are owed. Requests arrive here and you accept or decline them.

  • Set your own services and prices
  • Block days without declining work
  • See each request's time, place and group before you accept
  • Renewal reminders before a licence lapses

Android, in testing. iOS to follow.

Not on the stores yet

Both apps are in closed testing on Android. If you want to plan a trip in the meantime,use the web trip planner.Guides and drivers can apply on the web, and we will call to set up the app with you.
