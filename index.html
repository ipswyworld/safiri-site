What a licence check actually means

Most marketplaces show you a badge that the platform invented and awarded to itself. Ours points at a document with a number on it, issued by somebody who can take it away.

Tour guide licence

Tourism Regulatory Authority

Issued by the Tourism Regulatory Authority. Without a current one, a guide cannot take any guiding booking on Safiri at all.

PSV badge and passenger insurance

NTSA

NTSA licenses anyone carrying passengers for payment. We check the badge and the insurance behind it before a driver can accept an airport run.

Certificate of good conduct

Directorate of Criminal Investigations

From the Directorate of Criminal Investigations, and required before anyone can be booked by a traveller travelling alone.

And we record the expiry dates

A licence that lapses quietly is the real risk. Safiri records every document's expiry date and checks it again when it is renewed.

The park fee problem

Ask what a day at Nairobi National Park costs and you will usually be quoted the guide's fee. Then you reach the gate and there is another 5,200 shillings a head, set by Kenya Wildlife Service and revised seasonally, that nobody mentioned.

Safiri lists what a guide charges, what it includes, and what it does not, on the same screen. Every guide writes their own exclusions. We are not going to pretend a park fee is our money to waive, but we are not going to let it ambush you either.

Ready to be checked?

Bring your licence and your national ID, and say what you offer. We confirm the rest with the issuing bodies.
