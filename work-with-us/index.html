Your licence is worth more when it is checked

You did the training and you keep the paperwork current. On most platforms that counts for nothing, because anyone can type "expert local guide" into a profile. On Safiri we check every document with the body that issued it, and travellers see the proof.

Apply to SafiriWhat we check

You set your prices

Not one day rate that the app slices up. List a full day, a half day, an airport run, a three-day safari, each priced the way you actually price it, with your own party limits and your own exclusions.

You control your calendar

Block a day and requests stop arriving for it. No declining work you were never available for, and no acceptance rate punishing you for a funeral.

You see what you keep

For now travellers pay you directly, and Safiri takes nothing. Before Safiri ever handles your money, your terms, including any commission, are agreed with you in writing, and the app shows what you keep before you accept a booking.

You are not competing with unlicensed people

Nobody appears in search until their documents are confirmed. That is the whole premise, and it is why your licence counts for something here.

What you will need

It depends on what you do. Checks usually take two working days.

Guides
Tour guide licence from the Tourism Regulatory Authority. A KPSGA grade if you hold one, which puts you into safari and park searches. National ID, and a certificate of good conduct.
Drivers
PSV badge from NTSA, passenger insurance, national ID, and a certificate of good conduct.
Hosts and translators
National ID and a certificate of good conduct.

Safiri never publishes your licence number. Travellers see that a document was checked, who issued it, and that it is still current.

Apply to Safiri

Applying is not joining. You will not appear to travellers until we have checked your documents, and you upload them in the Safiri Partner app after you apply.
